WrenchWorks Garage: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

WrenchWorks Garage is a free car repair and street racing game made by Ezra Strickland. This page explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking, and the developer does not collect or sell your personal information.

Information saved on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your own device, in your browser's or app's local storage. This includes your garage, cars, parts, money, settings, achievements, the name you choose for online play, your friend code and your friends list. This information is not sent to the developer. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data for the game, uninstalling the app, or using the game's reset option.

Online play

Online play (drag races, car show battles, garage co-op and friends) is optional. When you use it:

  • The game connects your device directly to your friend's device. To set up the connection, it uses a free public connection service called PeerJS (peerjs.com), which passes along a temporary connection ID and the room code or friend code you are using.
  • While two devices are connected, each one can see the other's internet (IP) address, as is normal for direct online connections.
  • The other player sees the name you typed, your car and its parts, and your race and show results. Please do not use your real full name or any personal details as your player name.
  • There is no open chat, and you can only play with someone who has your room code or friend code.

The developer does not run these connection servers, and does not store or see your online games. The PeerJS service has its own privacy terms.

Game files from other websites

The game loads some free code libraries from public content delivery networks (cdnjs.cloudflare.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net and unpkg.com), and the web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the files are downloaded.

What the game does not do

  • No sign-in or accounts.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

WrenchWorks Garage is intended for players aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
